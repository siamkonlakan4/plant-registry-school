# Label guide

100 × 150 mm at design coordinate 400 × 600 pixels; 120 × 180 mm uses same aspect ratio. Keep essential text 3 mm inside trim edge; QR at least 25 × 25 mm with white quiet zone and black modules. Test printed QR at physical size before installing. A4 with 5 mm margins and 4 mm gutters fits two 100 × 150 mm labels. Print at 100% / actual size, disable browser header/footer.
